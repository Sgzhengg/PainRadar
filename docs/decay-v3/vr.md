## 典型叙述

### 1. 无线PC VR游戏

> I cannot upgrade my WiFi in a way that enables me to play wirelessly, I just can’t that is not realistic for me right now

- 期待：无线玩PC VR游戏
- 实际：WiFi无法支持无线，USB link不稳定，虚拟桌面不兼容且更差
- 触发：`friction`　替代：link（有线）　处置：`still_using`

### 2. 多人观看2D电影

> Big Screen was overly complicated to launch for multiplayer viewing and even on LAN performance was terrible

- 期待：简单启动多人观看
- 实际：启动复杂，局域网性能差
- 触发：`friction`　替代：unknown　处置：`gave_up`

### 3. 使用Rift S玩Steam VR游戏

> Relinked VR is a decent solution to ditching the meta app

- 期待：摆脱Meta软件，稳定运行Steam VR
- 实际：Relinked VR可用但缺少Guardian、透视和电量信息
- 触发：`ecosystem_lock`　替代：Relinked VR + Stop Sign VR　处置：`still_using`

### 4. 模拟赛车时看清 peripheral 设备

> I stopped using the facial interface a year ago for sim racing

- 期待：保持沉浸感
- 实际：去掉面罩后更沉浸、不闷热、不干扰
- 触发：`discomfort`　替代：无面罩使用　处置：`still_using`
